SCOPE IS PART OF THE WORKFLOW

Know the boundary.
Keep the context.

ParameterSniper sends HTTP requests, including parameter variations and requests to common paths. Use it only on systems you own or have explicit permission to assess; prefer an isolated lab.

Its response indicators are heuristic signals for human review, not proof of a vulnerability or a guarantee of security. Review the source and operational impact, define a narrow scope, and handle exported reports as sensitive data.
